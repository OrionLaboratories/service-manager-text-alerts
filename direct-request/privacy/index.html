Client Services direct-request privacy policy

Orion Laboratories uses the work identity, mobile number, dated request and delivery or opt-out status to verify, send and support the requested test. The release operator handles the request privately. Only the information needed to transmit and support the message is processed by the messaging provider and carriers.

Orion does not sell mobile numbers or share mobile information, text messaging opt-in data or consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The test uses synthetic cases. Keep patient information out of requests, texts and replies.

Reply STOP to opt out. Contact help@orion.healthcare for help or questions about the use of your information.
