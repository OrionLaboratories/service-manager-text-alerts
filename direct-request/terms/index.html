Client Services direct-request SMS terms

Orion Laboratories offers participating employees one automated Client Services test text per direct request. Each message identifies a synthetic case, an action and an authenticated Salesforce link. A request applies only to the employee’s own mobile number and does not enroll the employee in recurring alerts.

Participation is optional. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Your carrier may deliver a standard keyword confirmation. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. For help, contact help@orion.healthcare.
