Orion Laboratories work text alert terms

Orion Laboratories offers recurring automated Orion Service Manager case-assignment, coverage and escalation texts to employees who voluntarily enroll using the work text alert consent form. Each alert includes the case number, the action and a link that requires a Salesforce sign-in. The program excludes marketing, recruiting and patient messages.

Message frequency varies with case activity. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Reply STOP to end alerts. You may receive one opt-out confirmation. Reply HELP for assistance or email help@orion.healthcare. Previously enrolled employees who wish to resume may contact support and reply START after enrollment is confirmed. A signed form is required for first-time enrollment.

Orion keeps bell and email notice options available to employees who decline text alerts. See the SMS privacy policy.
