Orion Laboratories work text alert privacy policy

This policy applies to the Orion Service Manager employee text alert program. Orion Laboratories uses the mobile number, name, signed consent, consent date and opt-out record to administer the program, send requested alerts, respond to help requests and honor withdrawals. Signed forms are retained privately in the employee's personnel record. The public form is a blank copy.

Orion does not sell or share mobile numbers, text-message opt-in data, or consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Orion limits processing by messaging providers and carriers to what is needed to deliver and support this program. Access to enrollment records is limited to authorized staff. Case alert messages contain the case number, action and authenticated link. Keep patient details and other confidential information out of replies and support email.

Reply STOP to withdraw or contact help@orion.healthcare about participation or your enrollment information. See the SMS terms.
